DengShell RDP preview bundles FreeRDP 3.32.0 (Apache-2.0), OpenSSL 3.6.4 (Apache-2.0), and MinGW runtime components. FreeRDP source: https://github.com/FreeRDP/FreeRDP/tree/3.32.0
Modified Windows client: lifecycle events, embedded viewport, input focus isolation, secure-attention button, concise certificate fingerprint display, no Windows credential-store lookup.
